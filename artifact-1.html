Artifact Title

AI & ML Timeline from Residency

02

Introduction

This artifact presents the AI and machine learning timeline developed from the residency learning experience. It summarizes major stages in the evolution of artificial intelligence and connects those stages to modern enterprise use.

03

Description

Objective

To organize key milestones in AI/ML development in a clear timeline and demonstrate how AI evolved from fixed rule-based systems to learning systems, deep learning, generative AI, and enterprise applications.

Process

  1. Reviewed the AI/ML concepts introduced during residency.
  2. Organized the concepts into chronological stages.
  3. Summarized the defining capability of each stage.
  4. Connected the timeline to modern business and data applications.
  5. Presented the result in a concise digital portfolio format.

AI & ML Timeline

1950s–1980s

Rule-Based Artificial Intelligence

Early AI systems used predefined rules and decision logic. They could solve structured problems but had limited ability to learn from new information.

1990s–2000s

Machine Learning

AI systems increasingly learned patterns from historical data, supporting classification, forecasting, fraud detection, and process optimization.

2010s

Deep Learning and Big Data

Greater computing power and larger datasets enabled advances in image recognition, natural language processing, and complex pattern detection.

2020s

Generative AI and Intelligent Assistants

Generative AI enabled systems to create text, images, code, summaries, and recommendations and to support conversational experiences.

Today

AI Integrated with Enterprise Data

Organizations increasingly combine AI with governed data platforms, analytics, automation, and human expertise to improve decisions and business outcomes.

05

Value Proposition of the Artifact

Unique Value

The timeline converts a broad history of AI into a simple progression that makes the relationship among major AI/ML approaches easy to understand.

Relevance

This artifact is relevant to AI/ML coursework because it demonstrates foundational knowledge needed to understand current models, tools, and applications. It also satisfies the Workshop 5 requirement that Artifact 1 be the AI & ML Timeline from Residency.

06

References

Residency and course learning materials used to develop the AI/ML timeline. No additional external references are required for the portfolio summary.
